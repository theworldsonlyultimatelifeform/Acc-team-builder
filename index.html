Reference pictures (base art and skins)

The scanner compares each card to these pictures, so add one for the base art and one for every skin. Use screenshots of the whole card the way it looks in your inventory. You can pick many files at once if they’re named like fox_sin__halloween.png (card name, two underscores, skin name) or fox_sin__base.png.

Move your reference pictures to another device: copy the pack, then paste it there and tap Load. Leave the box empty and tap Load to pick a saved pack file.

Your cards are saved in this browser only. Copy the backup code to keep it, or paste a code to load it on another device. Card pictures stay on this device and aren’t included in the code.

Teams

A normal deck is 4 attackers + 4 supports. Each attacker gets one support.

How teams are picked

Each attacker is scored by its real damage for the version you own (Gold is 4× Basic, Rainbow 16×, Secret 64×), plus its trait. Cards ranked on the community tier lists (version 7.2) get a bonus for strong abilities. Where a card's stats haven't been added yet, the site estimates them and marks them “est.”.

Supports are ranked by tier. Survival supports go on your strongest attacker. Supports that trigger when their card falls, like Fallen Knight and Truck-Kun, go on a weaker card placed near the top, so the cards after it get the benefit.

Privacy

You don’t need an account. Without signing in, everything you add stays in your own browser.

If you sign in with Google, the site saves your card list and team size to your account so they load on your other devices. Google sign-in is run by Google’s Firebase, which keeps your Google account’s email and ID so you can sign in. Card pictures and scans never leave your device, and nothing is shared or sold. You can delete your saved data at any time with the button below.
